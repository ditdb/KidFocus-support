# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective: 2026-11-06

## 中文

**KidFocus（定心宝）不收集、不上传、不出售任何数据。** 应用没有账号、没有服务器、没有网络请求，也没有任何第三方 SDK、广告或分析工具。

### 数据保存在哪里
所有内容只保存在你的这台 iPad 上：

- **专注规则**（名称、时间、重复日、模式）和你选择的 App：保存在本机的应用容器里。App 的选择以系统提供的不透明令牌表示，KidFocus 无法读取任何 App 的名称或 Bundle ID。
- **家长密码**：不保存明文，只保存加盐哈希，存放在本机钥匙串（不随备份迁移）。
- **设置与门禁状态**（防护开关、错误次数、是否启用面容 ID / 触控 ID）：保存在本机。
- **诊断记录**：最近的屏蔽开关事件，保存在本机，不含任何 App 令牌。只有你点「导出诊断信息」并自己选择发送对象时，才会离开设备。

### 使用的系统权限
- **屏幕使用时间（Family Controls）**：用于在你设定的时段屏蔽 App。数据由系统处理，KidFocus 不会读取孩子的使用记录。
- **面容 ID / 触控 ID**（可选）：仅用于在本机验证家长身份。KidFocus 拿不到任何生物特征数据，只得到系统返回的「通过 / 未通过」。

### 儿童
KidFocus 由家长使用和配置，不向儿童收集任何信息。

### 删除数据
删除 KidFocus 即清除其保存的全部规则与设置。钥匙串里的密码摘要会在重新安装后首次启动时被清除。

### 变更与联系
本政策如有变化，会在此页面更新。问题请联系：<ditdb.team@gmail.com>

## English

**KidFocus does not collect, upload or sell any data.** There are no accounts, no servers, no network requests, and no third-party SDKs, ads or analytics.

### Where your data lives
Everything stays on this iPad:

- **Focus rules** (name, times, repeat days, mode) and your app selection. Selections are opaque system tokens; KidFocus cannot read any app's name or bundle ID.
- **Parent passcode**: never stored in plain text. Only a salted hash is kept in the on-device Keychain (not migrated by backups).
- **Settings and gate state** (protection switches, failed-attempt count, Face ID / Touch ID preference): stored on the device.
- **Diagnostics**: a short log of recent blocking events, stored on the device and containing no app tokens. It leaves the device only if you tap "Export Diagnostics" and choose where to send it.

### System permissions
- **Screen Time (Family Controls)**: used to block apps during the windows you set. The system handles the data; KidFocus does not read your child's usage.
- **Face ID / Touch ID** (optional): used only to verify the parent on this device. KidFocus never receives biometric data, only a pass/fail result from the system.

### Children
KidFocus is configured and used by parents and collects no information from children.

### Deleting your data
Deleting KidFocus removes all rules and settings it stores. The passcode hash in the Keychain is cleared on the first launch after a reinstall.

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: <ditdb.team@gmail.com>
